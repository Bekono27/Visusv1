01 — The story
"She runs marathons. She can't see without glasses. No product existed for her."
— Beck, Founder of VISUS

My girlfriend runs. She has run through cities, trails, and marathons across Vietnam. She also has bad eyesight. And the problem nobody has solved — until now — is that visually impaired athletes are completely ignored by both the corrective eyewear industry and the sports technology industry at the same time.

She can wear prescription glasses or she can wear a smart watch or she can wear sport sunglasses. She cannot wear all three in one device. She has to squint at a tiny watch face mid-sprint. She has to stop to check her GPS. She cannot see clearly and see her data at the same time.

VISUS changes that. For her. For every athlete who needs correction. For everyone who has ever wished their data was exactly where their eyes already are.

08 — Questions
Answered.
Do I need a prescription to use VISUS?
No. VISUS works with or without prescription lenses. If you have a prescription, you submit it during the order process and we surface both lens sets (tinted and clear) to your exact correction. If you don't need correction, plano (zero power) lenses are included at no extra cost.
What prescription range does VISUS support?
VISUS supports sphere -6.00 to +4.00 diopter and cylinder up to +/-2.00 diopter. This covers the vast majority of prescription users. If you are outside this range, please contact us before backing and we will confirm compatibility.
How does the HUD work? Can I see through it?
The HUD uses a waveguide microLED system — a tiny projector in the temple arm fires light into the lens, which carries it to your eye via a diffractive optical element etched into the polycarbonate. The display appears as a transparent overlay. You see the real world clearly, with data floating in your lower-right field of view. In minimal mode the lens is completely clear.
What apps does VISUS work with?
At launch: Spotify, Google Maps, Apple Maps, WhatsApp, iMessage, Strava, Apple Health, and Google Health Connect. VISUS is an open platform — we are releasing a third-party SDK so any developer can build a lens widget. More integrations will be added based on backer requests.
What is the Performance subscription?
The free tier includes basic HUD, GPS, HR, music control, and navigation. The Performance subscription ($15/month) adds the full Performance OS — recovery score, readiness, HRV, sleep analysis, AI real-time coaching, training load, AI agent full commands, and longitudinal health insights. Early Bird and Bundle backers receive 6–12 months free.
When will VISUS ship?
Alpha units (50 units) ship to Early Bird backers at Month 8. Full production ships at Month 14. We will give backers regular transparent updates throughout the build process. Hardware is hard and timelines can shift — we will always communicate early if they do.
What if the campaign does not reach its goal?
Kickstarter is all-or-nothing. If we do not reach $50,000, no one is charged and no money changes hands. We believe we will reach the goal — but if we don't, backers are fully protected.
