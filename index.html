01 — The story
"She runs marathons. She can't see without glasses. No product in the world existed for her."
— Beck, Founder · visus.life · Hanoi, Vietnam

My girlfriend runs. She has run marathons across Vietnam — cities, trails, mountains. She also has bad eyesight. And the problem nobody has solved — until now — is that visually impaired athletes are completely ignored by both the corrective eyewear industry and the sports technology industry at the same time.

She has to squint at a tiny watch face mid-sprint. She cannot see her GPS clearly without stopping. She cannot wear prescription glasses that also show her data. She has to choose between seeing the world clearly and seeing her performance data.

VISUS gives her both. And every athlete who needs it.

10 — Questions
Answered.
Is the wrist wrap included in every bundle?
Yes — every reward tier from $299 upwards includes the VISUS wrist wrap in the box. There is no glasses-only option at backer pricing. The wrist wrap is what powers the Performance OS recovery score, sleep analysis, and HRV tracking — without it, the morning dashboard doesn't work.
Do I need a prescription to use VISUS?
No. VISUS works with or without prescription lenses. If you have a prescription, you submit it during the order process and we surface both lens sets to your exact correction. If you don't need correction, plano (zero power) lenses are included at no extra cost. Supported range: sphere -6.00 to +4.00, cylinder +/-2.00.
How does the HUD work? Can I see through it?
The HUD uses a MicroLED waveguide system — a tiny projector in the temple arm fires light into the polycarbonate lens, which propagates it to your eye via a diffractive optical element etched into the lens. The display appears as a transparent overlay. You see the real world clearly with data floating in your peripheral vision. In minimal mode the lens is fully clear.
What apps does VISUS work with?
At launch: Spotify, Google Maps, Apple Maps, WhatsApp, iMessage, Strava, Apple Health, and Google Health Connect. VISUS is an open platform — we release a third-party SDK so any developer can build a lens widget. The wrist wrap syncs health data to all major health platforms automatically.
What is the Performance subscription?
The free tier includes basic HUD, GPS, HR, music control, and navigation. The Performance subscription ($15/month) adds the full Performance OS powered by your wrist wrap data — recovery score, readiness, HRV, sleep analysis, AI real-time coaching, training load, stress monitoring, and AI agent full commands. Early Bird backers receive 6 months free.
Is VISUS compatible with helmets and cycling?
Yes. The 2mm blade temple profile is specifically designed for helmet compatibility — the ultra-thin arms slide under helmet retention systems without pressure points. Cycling mode includes speed, cadence, navigation, and HR monitoring. The wrist wrap continues tracking when the glasses are off.
When does VISUS ship?
Alpha units (50 units) ship to Early Bird backers at Month 8. Full production ships at Month 14. We update backers transparently throughout the build. Hardware is hard and timelines can shift — we commit to communicating early if they do.
What if the campaign doesn't reach its goal?
Kickstarter is all-or-nothing. If we don't reach $50,000, no one is charged and no money changes hands. Backers are fully protected.
